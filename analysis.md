## Problem Decomposition

1. **Input:** Price, Quantity, Member
2. **Process:** Validasi -> Subtotal -> Discount -> Total (Subtotal After Discount) -> Shipping -> Total Payment
3. **Output:** Subtotal, Discount, Shipping, Total Payment OR "Input tidak valid"
4. **Pattern:** Rumus Subtotal, Discount, dan Total Payment
5. **Abstraction:** Foto dan warna produk
